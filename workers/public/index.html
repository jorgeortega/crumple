These keys are public on purpose. They reach fake data, and neither can delete. To let an agent charge fake customers too, use this key instead:

crumple_public-demo-charge-key-fake-payments-only-1
Why might a payment vanish?

Everyone shares these keys, and the data lives in memory on whichever server answers. It resets every hour, so a payment can seem to vanish between calls. The cards above each get their own fresh copy, so that's where the one-charge result comes from. Nothing can make this endpoint fail on purpose, because a planted instruction shouldn't reach a stranger's agent.

    What this is, and isn't

    • A demo, not a load test. A card shows one run, and says nothing about production.
    • Everything is fake. No model runs here.
    • Rate limited: about 60 requests a minute per address and 20,000 a day in total. Past that it answers with an error until the limit resets.
    • No cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Your address is only used for the rate limit, and isn't stored.
